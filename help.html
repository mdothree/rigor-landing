Answers about Rigor Career, seven AI-assisted career tools, and where to reach us.

What is Rigor Career?

Seven AI-assisted career tools, each on its own page. Every tool is currently labelled Beta.

  • Resume Analyzer
  • Cover Letter
  • Portfolio Review
  • LinkedIn Optimizer
  • Networking Emails
  • Interview Prep
  • Salary Negotiator

Which tool first?

Start where you are in the search: the Resume Analyzer, Cover Letter and Portfolio Review help you apply; LinkedIn Optimizer and Networking Emails help you reach out; Interview Prep and Salary Negotiator come after you have a conversation.

What does it cost?

Pro plans are paid. Where a tool has one, its price is shown on that tool’s page before you commit.

Contact, billing and refunds

For account, billing or any other question, use the contact page. Rigor Career is an MDO3D product; the policies that apply are on MDO3D: Refunds, Terms and Privacy.
